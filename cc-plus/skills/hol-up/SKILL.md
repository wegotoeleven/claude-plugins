# Hol Up

Re-explain your own last response in plain language, as if for an intelligent adult with no background in the topic.

## Steps

1. Identify your immediately preceding assistant message — that's the material to re-explain, not the user's message that triggered this skill.
2. Strip out jargon, acronyms, and technical terms. Where a technical term is unavoidable, swap in a plain-language description the first time it's used instead of defining it separately afterward.
3. Lead with one sentence that captures the core point, then support it with a single concrete, everyday analogy.
4. One idea per sentence. Prefer short, direct sentences over compound ones.
5. Cut anything not needed to understand the core point — ruthless simplification beats completeness. 80% accuracy with full comprehension beats 100% accuracy that loses the reader.
6. Replace the technical version, don't append the plain one after it "for context."
7. If the last message contained code or commands, explain what it does and why it matters, not its syntax.

## Tone

Never condescending. Simple isn't childish — write for someone sharp who just lacks the domain background, not someone who needs to be talked down to.
